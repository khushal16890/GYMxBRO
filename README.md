GYMxBRO
A comprehensive fitness platform designed to simplify workout tracking and health education. This application provides users with personalized tools to reach their fitness goals through data-driven insights and expert content.

Features
Custom Workout Plans: Build and manage your own training routines tailored to your specific schedule.

AI-Powered Plans: Generate optimized workout programs based on your fitness level and available equipment using integrated AI.

Exercise Database: Access a detailed library of exercises with proper form instructions and guidance.

Learning Blogs: Stay informed with educational articles covering nutrition, recovery, and training science.
